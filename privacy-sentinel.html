IS Studio® — Sentinel

Privacy Policy
for Sentinel

Effective date: July 15, 2026

Sentinel is operated by IS Studio, located in Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinel collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the Sentinel application, website, or services, including features connected to LinkedIn. By using Sentinel or connecting your LinkedIn account, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information received from LinkedIn

When you connect your LinkedIn account to Sentinel, LinkedIn may provide us with information based on the permissions you approve.

This information may include:

  • Your LinkedIn member ID
  • Your name
  • Your profile picture
  • Your email address
  • Your LinkedIn profile information
  • LinkedIn Pages or organizations you manage
  • Page posts, comments, reactions, mentions, or analytics
  • Advertising account or campaign information, when applicable
  • The permissions granted to Sentinel
  • LinkedIn access tokens needed to maintain the connection

Sentinel only requests information and permissions that are reasonably necessary to provide the selected features.

Information you provide directly

We may collect information that you submit to Sentinel, including:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Account information
  • Business or company information
  • Content you create, upload, schedule, or publish
  • Customer-support messages
  • Settings and preferences

Technical information

When you use Sentinel, we may automatically receive limited technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Login and activity timestamps
  • Application error reports
  • Security and diagnostic logs
  • Pages or features used within Sentinel

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your Sentinel account
  • Authenticate you through LinkedIn
  • Connect Sentinel to LinkedIn Pages or accounts you authorize
  • Display LinkedIn information inside Sentinel
  • Publish, schedule, edit, or manage content when requested by you
  • Display comments, reactions, mentions, or analytics
  • Provide reports and performance information
  • Maintain account settings and integrations
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or security incidents
  • Maintain and improve Sentinel
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Enforce our agreements and protect our legal rights

Sentinel will not publish content, send messages, or perform actions through your LinkedIn account unless you have requested or enabled the relevant feature.

3. LinkedIn Authentication

Sentinel uses LinkedIn’s OAuth authorization process.

Your LinkedIn username and password are entered directly through LinkedIn. Sentinel does not receive or store your LinkedIn password.

LinkedIn provides Sentinel with an authorization code or access token after you approve the requested permissions. Sentinel uses that token only to provide the LinkedIn-connected features you authorize.

You can remove Sentinel’s access through your LinkedIn account settings or by contacting us.

4. How We Share Information

Sentinel does not sell or rent personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate Sentinel, such as:

  • Cloud-hosting providers
  • Database and storage providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Email and communication providers
  • Analytics and error-monitoring providers
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers

These providers may process information only to perform services for Sentinel and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We may also disclose information:

  • When required by law, regulation, court order, or legal process
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • To protect Sentinel, IS Studio, our users, or the public
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of the business
  • When you give us permission to do so

5. LinkedIn Data

Information obtained from LinkedIn is used only in connection with Sentinel’s approved LinkedIn features and in accordance with applicable LinkedIn API terms.

Sentinel will:

  • Request only the permissions needed for its features
  • Use LinkedIn information only for disclosed purposes
  • Protect LinkedIn access tokens from unauthorized access
  • Respect applicable LinkedIn data-storage and caching restrictions
  • Delete LinkedIn-derived information when required by LinkedIn’s terms
  • Respond to valid user deletion and access requests

Certain LinkedIn information relating to people who have not authenticated directly with Sentinel may only be temporarily cached and may be subject to shorter storage limits.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide Sentinel
  • Maintain your account and authorized integrations
  • Fulfil the purposes described in this policy
  • Meet legal, accounting, security, or regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

LinkedIn access tokens are retained while your LinkedIn connection remains active or until the token expires, is revoked, or is no longer needed.

When you disconnect LinkedIn, close your Sentinel account, or submit a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information unless retention is required by law, security obligations, LinkedIn requirements, or legitimate record-keeping needs.

Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups are securely overwritten.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

These measures may include:

  • Encryption during transmission
  • Restricted access to production systems
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Secure storage of access tokens and credentials
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Software updates and vulnerability management
  • Backup and recovery procedures

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and notify us immediately if they believe their account has been accessed without permission.

8. International Data Processing

Sentinel and its service providers may process or store information in Canada, the United States, or other countries where our service providers operate.

Information processed in another country may be subject to the laws and lawful access requirements of that country.

Where required, we use reasonable contractual and security safeguards for international processing.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Withdraw consent
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request information about how your information has been used or disclosed
  • Disconnect your LinkedIn account
  • Submit a privacy complaint

To protect users, we may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Some requests may be limited where retaining or processing the information is required by law or necessary to protect legal rights, security, or other users.

10. Withdrawing LinkedIn Access

You may stop Sentinel’s access to your LinkedIn account by:

  • Disconnecting LinkedIn inside Sentinel, when that option is available.
  • Removing Sentinel through your LinkedIn account’s permitted-services or application settings.
  • Contacting IS Studio and requesting disconnection or deletion.

Revoking access prevents future LinkedIn API access but may not automatically delete information already stored by Sentinel. You may separately request deletion by contacting us.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Sentinel may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to:

  • Keep users signed in
  • Maintain security
  • Remember preferences
  • Operate essential application features
  • Understand application performance
  • Diagnose technical problems

Where required, Sentinel will request consent before using non-essential cookies or analytics technologies.

12. Children’s Privacy

Sentinel is designed for businesses and professional users. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Third-Party Services

Sentinel may contain links to or integrations with third-party services, including LinkedIn.

Those services maintain their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own privacy practices. This Privacy Policy does not control how LinkedIn or other third parties collect and process information through their own services.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sentinel’s features, legal obligations, or privacy practices change.

When changes are significant, we may provide notice through Sentinel, by email, or through our website.

The effective date at the top of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.

Questions, privacy requests, deletion requests, or complaints may be sent to:

Privacy Officer
IS Studio – Sentinel
Ontario, Canada
